Scope & Limitations

What LAN Sentinel covers, and where its boundaries are.

Scope

What the system will do.

Single-LAN monitoring

Monitors traffic within one local area network, including its Wi-Fi segment, through passive packet capture (for example a mirrored port or a monitoring host).

ML-based anomaly detection

Identifies unauthorized devices and abnormal traffic patterns such as port scanning, ARP spoofing and flooding.

Web-based dashboard

Alerts, logs and basic reports for the administrator.

Controlled testing

Tests run in a controlled environment using simulated attacks and publicly available datasets.

Limitations

What the system will not do.

Detect and alert only

The system will not automatically block devices or traffic (no intrusion prevention).

No encrypted payload inspection

Analysis relies on traffic metadata and behavior, not on inspecting encrypted data.

Small to medium LANs

May not scale to large enterprise or ISP-level networks.

Depends on training data

Accuracy depends on how representative the data is; a model trained on one network may need retraining before use on another.

Limited attack coverage

Evaluation is limited to the attack types simulated in the testing environment and may not cover all possible threats.

Wireless-layer hardware limits

Attacks that need specialized radio monitoring hardware (for example deauthentication floods) are limited to what the available equipment can capture.

LAN Sentinel

An AI-based Network Intrusion Detection System for Local Area Networks using Traffic Anomaly Analysis.
