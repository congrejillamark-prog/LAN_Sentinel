The problem

Why small networks stay exposed

Wi-Fi makes shared resources easy to reach, and it also makes unauthorized access easier to attempt.

Prevention is not monitoring

Wi-Fi passwords, firewalls and MAC filtering follow fixed rules. They do not continuously watch behavior, and MAC addresses can be cloned.

Signature tools need expertise

Snort and Suricata match known attack patterns, so new or modified attacks can slip by, and rules need regular tuning.

Few people, small budgets

Schools and small offices often have no dedicated security staff or affordable monitoring tools.

How it works

From raw packets to a clear alert

1
Capture

Passive packet capture on the LAN, for example through a mirrored port or a monitoring host.

2
Extract features

Flow-level and device-level features: packet rates, packet sizes, protocol mix and MAC/IP activity.

3
Learn & detect

A machine learning model learns the baseline of normal behavior and flags deviations as anomalies.

4
Alert

Anomalies and unauthorized devices appear on a web dashboard with logs and basic reports.

Built for small networks

Designed for administrators without a security team

LAN Sentinel pairs the ease of use of device scanners with anomaly-based threat detection, in one lightweight tool.

  • Device discovery and unknown-device alerts
  • Machine learning baseline for one specific LAN
  • Detection of port scans, ARP spoofing and flooding
  • Real-time alerts, event history and basic reports
  • Low-cost: no expensive licenses or specialized hardware
